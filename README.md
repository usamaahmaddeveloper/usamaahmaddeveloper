## About Me

Hi — I’m **Usama Ahmad Developer** (`@usamaahmaddeveloper`, he/him), a full-stack developer based in **Multan, Punjab, Pakistan**, and **CTO at [Titan Growths](https://titangrowths.com)**.

I specialize in building production web platforms and business software — from CMS-driven websites and e-commerce storefronts to custom **Odoo 19** modules for product pages, logistics, and marketplace workflows.

My focus is practical engineering: clean modules, reliable integrations, and features that hold up in real storefront and shipping environments.
