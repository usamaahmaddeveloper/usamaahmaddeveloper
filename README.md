## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/People/Man%20Technologist.png" alt="" width="28" /> About

I'm **Usama Ahmad Developer** (`@usamaahmaddeveloper`) — full-stack engineer and **CTO at [Titan Growths](https://titangrowths.com)**, based in **Pakistan**.

I design and ship business-critical systems across three layers:

| Layer | What I deliver |
|:---|:---|
| **Commerce platforms** | CMS & storefront builds (WordPress, Shopify) with conversion-focused UX |
| **ERP / Odoo** | Odoo 19 enterprise modules for PDP, wishlist, logistics, and marketplace workflows |
| **AI systems** | Multi-agent research platforms with FastAPI, LangGraph, and sandboxed tool development |

My engineering style is practical and production-first: clean module boundaries, reliable integrations, documented workflows, and features that survive real storefront and shipping traffic.
